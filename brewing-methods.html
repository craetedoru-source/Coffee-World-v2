Brewing Methods

Choose the method that suits your taste

This page explains common brewing methods in a simple way so users can understand which option may fit their experience level and taste preference.

French Press

Rich body and simple process. Great for beginners.

Best for: Medium and dark roast coffees

Espresso Machine

Strong, concentrated coffee with a more advanced setup.

Best for: Dark or espresso roast beans

Pour Over

Clean flavour and high control over extraction.

Best for: Light and medium roast beans

Filter Coffee

Reliable and convenient for regular daily use.

Best for: Balanced medium roasts
