About Coffee World

A user-centred coffee information website

This page explains the purpose of the project and the design goals behind the website.

Project Purpose

Coffee World was designed as a computing project artefact using HTML and CSS. The website was created to help people enjoy the coffee they love from the comfort of their own home. During a cost-of-living crisis, regularly buying coffee from cafés can become expensive, but saving money should not mean giving up your favourite coffee or changing your preferences. Coffee World helps users understand different coffee beans, brewing methods and flavours so they can learn how to prepare coffee that suits their taste at home.

Design Goals

  • Simple navigation - make coffee information easy to find.
  • Responsive design - provide a good experience on desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • Clear visual content - use images and structured information to make coffee easier to understand.
  • Easy comparison - help users compare beans, flavours, caffeine levels and brewing methods.
  • Home brewing support - help users recreate the coffee they enjoy without relying on cafés.
