Our Story

Why Coffee World was created

Many coffee lovers enjoy drinking coffee but find it difficult to choose the right beans or brewing tools because information is spread across many websites and often written in a technical way.

Coffee World was created to solve this problem by bringing clear information, simple explanations and quick comparisons into one accessible website.

Our Aim

Simple guidance for better choices

The aim of Coffee World is to support users in understanding coffee by region, roast level, flavour notes, caffeine level and brewing method.

The website focuses on usability, clean design, responsive layout and easy navigation so users can find information quickly.

Project Objectives

What the website is designed to achieve

Improve clarity

Present coffee information in simple language for users who are not experts.

Support comparison

Help users compare beans, flavour notes, roast levels and brewing options.

Improve navigation

Use clear menus, page structure and shortcut cards to help users move around the site.

Be mobile friendly

Use a responsive layout so the website works on desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
