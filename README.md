The Coffee World project focuses on the design and development of a responsive, user-friendly website intended to help everyday coffee drinkers understand different types of coffee, their characteristics and the brewing methods used to prepare them. With a wide variety of coffee products and preparation methods available, beginners and intermediate users may find it difficult to identify suitable options or understand how different factors influence the final beverage. The project aims to address this problem by presenting relevant information through a clear, accessible and well-organised web-based platform.
